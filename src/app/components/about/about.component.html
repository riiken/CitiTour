About CitiTour

Trip planning from real places, for Indian travellers

CitiTour turns "where should we go and what should we do?" into a day-by-day plan you can trust, share and change.

What we do

  • Plans built from real places. We gather sights, stays and food from open travel guides (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap) and from businesses that list themselves with us. Our AI arranges them into days; it is not allowed to invent places.
  • Made to be changed. Save a trip, swap a stop, move things between days, and share the plan with your group on WhatsApp.
  • Local businesses, directly. Homestays, restaurants and guides list for free. We review each one, and you contact them directly.

How CitiTour is free

Some "Check prices" and "Book" links go to partners such as Booking.com or GetYourGuide, which may pay us a commission when you book, at no extra cost to you. Businesses can also pay for a Featured spot, which is always labelled "Sponsored". We never accept payment to change what our plans say about a place.

Please double-check

Prices, opening hours and availability change. Confirm them with the business or booking site before you travel.
